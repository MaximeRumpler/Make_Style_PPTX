# Every style on the same deck

| Style | Family | Use when | Theme | What it changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `clean` | Sober | Formal decks, boards, anything that must stay square; also the baseline to compare against | either | nothing: the baseline |
| `editorial` | Sober | Reports and talks with a point of view; a few slides laid out like a magazine page | light | arrangement: deconstructed, stagger |
| `swiss` | Sober | Technical, data-led or design-minded decks; precise, quiet | either | ambience: grid |
| `organic` | Organic | Warm, human subjects: wellbeing, culture, workshops | light | shapes: blob, circle, cloud, pebble, pill, rounded; ambience: mist |
| `paper` | Organic | Workshops, ideas, drafts, education; squared paper and torn sheets | light | shapes: handdrawn, ticket, torn; ambience: grid |
| `papercut` | Organic | Craft, stories and teaching; tactile, layered | light | shapes: ticket, torn; ambience: leak |
| `watercolour` | Organic | Gentle, creative or care-related subjects; arts, education, health | light | shapes: blob, circle, cloud, pebble, pill, rounded; ambience: wash |
| `blueprint` | Tech | Architecture, engineering, process and systems; precise drawn outlines | dark | shapes: handdrawn; ambience: grid |
| `tech-dark` | Tech | Product, engineering and security talks; modules and a grid | dark | shapes: pill, rounded; arrangement: cascade; ambience: grid |
| `terminal` | Tech | Developer and infrastructure audiences; steps stepping down like output | dark | shapes: rectangle; arrangement: cascade; ambience: grid |
| `bauhaus` | Expressive | Design and architecture; circles, blocks and a giant figure | light | shapes: circle, polygon; arrangement: deconstructed; giant word: background |
| `brutalist` | Expressive | Bold statements and manifestos; blunt blocks, a huge word on request | light | shapes: polygon, rectangle; arrangement: deconstructed, stagger; giant word: foreground |
| `collage` | Expressive | Creative, cultural or youth subjects; scraps pinned at different heights | light | shapes: bubble, ticket, torn; arrangement: stagger, cascade, arc; ambience: wash |
| `retro-70s` | Expressive | Brand, culture and storytelling with a warm, nostalgic tone | light | shapes: circle, pill, rounded; arrangement: arc; ambience: leak |
| `sketch` | Expressive | Brainstorms, discovery and early concepts; drawn by hand | light | shapes: handdrawn; arrangement: stagger |
| `nature` | Mood | Sustainability, outdoors, food and wellbeing; pebbles and a soft wash | light | shapes: blob, circle, cloud, pebble, pill, rounded; arrangement: stagger; ambience: wash |
| `rain` | Mood | Reflective, serious or melancholy subjects; falling items, thin rain | either | shapes: circle, cloud, drop, pebble, pill, rounded; arrangement: cascade; ambience: rain |
| `sunny` | Mood | Optimistic news, launches, summer events; open, round, warm light | light | shapes: circle, pill, rounded; arrangement: arc; ambience: leak |
